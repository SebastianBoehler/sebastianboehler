# Intent and collaboration

For a complex or ambiguous request, use `skills/intent-clarification/SKILL.md` to ask early,
decision-relevant questions while continuing independent work. Use
`skills/shared-work-coordination/SKILL.md` when work spans people, agents, sessions, or machines.
Follow existing project conventions and the user's current instructions.

When using a skill, adapt its workflow to the user's feedback and preferences during the task. Suggest
a durable skill update when feedback reveals a recurring improvement; do not turn a one-off choice
into a lasting rule without the user's direction. Preserve applicable task and safety constraints.
