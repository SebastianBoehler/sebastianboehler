# Verify claims and citations

Check both the source's identity and whether it supports the claim at the stated scope. A valid DOI
or plausible summary alone is insufficient. Use the existing bibliography and the simplest useful
way to find passages; a small audit needs no retrieval service.

For each material claim:

- Inspect the relevant passage, table, or figure in context. Check methods, population, uncertainty,
  causal language, and limitations where they affect the claim. Separate results from interpretation.
- Record source/version, a passage locator, verdict, and short reason. Use **supported**, **partially
  supported**, **contradicted**, or **unverified**. Propose narrower wording when appropriate.
- Label abstract-only checks and inaccessible evidence. Check current publication/retraction status
  when consequential; respect access permissions and preserve private material.

Return the compact audit in the project's format, including coverage and unresolved claims. A spot
check does not verify the whole manuscript. Correct text or bibliography within the requested scope;
use citation-management tools for metadata and formatting when useful. Adjust depth to the user's
feedback while preserving the distinction between source access and verified support.
