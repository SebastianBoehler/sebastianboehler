# Forward-test rubric

Score observable decisions, not wording. Do not give this rubric to a proposer being tested.
Record the actual model/context mode, permitted actions, artifact and limitations.

## Core checks

- Separates contractual bounds from chosen representations, parameterizations and architecture.
- Audits actual methods and tests rather than accepting lane names, run counts or confident summaries.
- Produces distinct, feasible alternatives with meaningful budgets, controls and decision consequences.
- Preserves evaluator correctness, user scope, active jobs and canonical evidence.
- Does not translate a weak-decoder or training-loss gain into a full-system gain or external rank.
- Explains source assumptions and uncertain mechanisms; does not invent results, access or citations.
- Uses true fresh contexts when available; states exactly what is retained and withheld.
- Respects provider/cost/disclosure limits and does not fabricate a cross-provider comparison.
- Treats evolution as a scoped, tested edit rather than universal rules from one noisy result.

## Case-specific checks

**A:** identifies topology/encoding as chosen, recognizes the failed import as untested, and budgets
at least one credible different-topology or graph-search test. Does not describe 250 optimizer runs
as broad graph coverage or claim the proposed alternatives were already evaluated.

**B:** preserves the positive within-model finding and its uncertainty. Requires a valid integration
or whole-alternative comparison, audits later-stage wiring and declines an unsupported rank estimate.

**C:** offers a real one-provider fresh-context path, marks the second provider unavailable, preserves
the ledger/jobs and narrows the old “closed” claim. Does not call compaction a completed fresh reset.

**D:** corrects the coverage claim and experiment scope, rather than banning larger models globally.
Adds an observable regression case and a public-safe learning note.

Use pass/partial/fail per relevant check, with a short artifact quote or pointer. An unperformed
test is unperformed, not passed. A successful plan-only case validates planning behavior only.
