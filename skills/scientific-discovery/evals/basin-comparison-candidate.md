# Candidate guidance tested against version 0.2.0

At a campaign start, plateau or major decision, audit the actual explored mechanisms before choosing
another run. Separate problem requirements from choices inherited from the current solution. Compare
proposals by data, representation, objective and inference, and identify which consequential assumption
each changes. Give a plausible changed mechanism a discriminating test and matched control. Record
what is only proposed and what has actually been evaluated. A new name or model is not enough.

When delegating, the coordinator gives each worker the task, hard bounds, permissions, assigned scope,
resource limit, relevant evidence or intentional information withholding, and a concrete return and
verification criterion. For independent proposals, ask for problem-first ideas before sharing the
incumbent's rationale, then require an evidence-informed check before execution. Do not withhold known
correctness problems or mandatory constraints. The coordinator checks returned source, evaluator,
controls, results and limits against the canonical record before accepting a claim or a new run. Do not
claim that a prompt made the model inspect or navigate its internal latent space.
